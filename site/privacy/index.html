Plain-language policy

Privacy

Effective August 28, 2026.

What the CLI collects

Nothing. Project Install Capsule has no telemetry, account, advertising identifier, or remote service. Configuration and teardown receipts stay on your machine. Approved network requests go directly from your machine to the hosts you name.

What this site collects

The site does not use cookies, analytics, fingerprinting, advertising, or third-party scripts. Your hosting provider may retain ordinary server access logs for security and operations. The interactive preview runs entirely in your browser and is not persisted.

Your choices

You can remove local receipts by deleting the project’s .capsule/receipts directory and remove the offline site cache through your browser settings.

Questions

Open an issue in the public source repository.
